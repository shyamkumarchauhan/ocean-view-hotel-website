About Ocean View School

Founded in 1999, Ocean View School has grown into a community of learners where academic excellence meets rich personal development.

We are dedicated to fostering creativity, critical thinking, and compassionate leadership among students of all ages.

Our curriculum is designed to adapt to individual student needs, nurturing strengths and addressing challenges in partnership with parents.
