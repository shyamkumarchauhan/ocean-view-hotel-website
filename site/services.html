Admissions

At Ocean View School, we invite parents and students to join our community. Our admissions process is transparent and focused on the holistic development of each child.

How to Apply

  1. Complete the online application or request a paper form.
  2. Participate in a school tour and meet our faculty.
  3. Schedule an assessment for your child.
  4. Receive admission decision and next steps guidance.

Programs Offered

Early Learning

Programs for preschool children focusing on creativity, social skills, and foundational knowledge.

Elementary School

Comprehensive learning for grades 1-5 with emphasis on core subjects and exploration.

Middle and High School

A rigorous curriculum preparing students for college and the challenges ahead.
